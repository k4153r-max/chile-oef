# Notices and external data

The BSD-3-Clause license covers CHILE-OEF source code only. It does not relicense
external earthquake, waveform, geodetic, tectonic, fault, or map data.

Every ingested artifact must retain its source, retrieval time, checksum, citation,
and applicable terms. CSN material must be attributed to the Centro Sismologico
Nacional de la Universidad de Chile. USGS-produced material is generally public
domain, but products contributed to ComCat can retain third-party terms.

No external production dataset is committed to this repository.
