# Limitations

- Short-horizon large-earthquake probabilities are usually small even during
  strongly elevated activity.
- Catalog detection and reporting change across time and space.
- Final revised catalogs cannot reconstruct historical source latency.
- USGS global coverage is not a substitute for the Chilean local network at low
  magnitudes.
- Slab2, Moho, fault maps, hypocenters, and focal mechanisms have uncertainty.
- Statistical clustering does not identify a unique physical triggering cause.
- A high IAS is not a high probability of a damaging earthquake.
- Occurrence forecasts are not forecasts of shaking, loss, or personal risk.
- M>=7 evaluation is sample limited and cannot support small-cell supervised ML.

Negative results, including failure of ML to improve ETAS, are valid scientific
outcomes and must be published without retrospective model adjustment.
